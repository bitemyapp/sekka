each with the outcome of reproducing it at `8e0acbe5`. The catalog's
[Suspected bugs](../implicit-state-machines.md#suspected-bugs) section says how
they were reproduced and what each status means. A linked ID has an entry
that gives the bug in full with its machine's states and hazards.
